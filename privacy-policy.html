When the website is accessed, the web server automatically records the following data and stores it in log files:

  • the IP address of the requesting device,
  • the date and time of the request,
  • the requested file or address (URL),
  • the HTTP status code and the amount of data transferred,
  • the previously visited page (referrer), if your browser transmits it,
  • the browser type and version as well as the operating system.

Purpose: Delivering the website, technically error-free operation and security (detecting and defending against attacks). Without processing the IP address, the website cannot technically be delivered.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1) sentence 1 point (f) GDPR. My legitimate interest lies in the secure and functional operation of the website.

Storage period: The log files are stored only as long as necessary for the stated purposes and are then deleted automatically. They are not merged with other data and not evaluated for statistics or advertising.

Pokémon Data and Images from Third-Party Providers

To display the Pokémon, your browser loads content directly from the servers of the following providers:

  • PokéAPI (pokeapi.co): the Pokémon data such as names, stats, types and moves. The PokéAPI is a free community project. To the best of my knowledge, it is delivered via content delivery networks, including Cloudflare, Inc. and Fastly, Inc. (both USA).
  • GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com): the Pokémon images from the PokeAPI/sprites repository. The provider is GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.

For technical reasons, these providers receive your IP address, the date and time of the request, the address requested, and browser and device information, and possibly also the address of this website as a referrer. I have no influence on the further processing by these providers. Information on this can be found in their privacy notices: GitHub, Cloudflare, Fastly.

Purpose: Displaying the Pokémon on this website. Without this content, the Pokédex cannot work.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1) sentence 1 point (f) GDPR. My legitimate interest is in providing you with the content of the Pokédex.

Transfer to the USA: The providers mentioned are based in the USA. Insofar as the provider is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, the transfer relies on the European Commission's adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR); otherwise it relies on standard contractual clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR).

No Cookies, No Tracking

This website does not set any cookies and does not store any information on your device (not even via Local Storage or Session Storage). There is no tracking and no audience measurement, and no analytics, advertising or social media services are embedded.
